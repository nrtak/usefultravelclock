Review of the new Home/navigation and existing travel tools, 2026-10-30.

Fixed:
- Home's trailing Spacer added a layout gap beyond the card budget. Removed it so the actual stack matches its capacity calculation.
- Home currency card omitted rate dates when online and could show a blank conversion result. Added the dated estimate and explicit unavailable result.
- Compact clocks lacked local dates, making date-line crossings ambiguous. Added local dates and stopped displaying UTC as a chosen destination when no city is selected.
- External document opening could dismiss a tool with unsaved work and could compete with another sheet. Routing waits for modals/editors to close. Multiple incoming files are queued with their security-scoped access retained until consumed. Invalid URLs and duplicate callbacks no longer replace pending imports.
- Weather city/search requests could finish out of order and replace a newer choice. A cancellable request scope rejects outdated completions and cancels work when the view disappears. Requests superseded before starting do not start.
- Simultaneous weather loads for the same city now share the in-progress request instead of returning before location resolution finishes.
- Weather tools open the destination forecast consistently with the Home card. Forecast freshness/hour/day filtering advances with time while visible; no network polling was added. Missing time-zone metadata explicitly labels forecast times as UTC.
- Live scanning could continue behind photo pickers or saved/edit sheets. Camera activity now includes a covered-screen condition; covered translation screens also cancel active live translation configuration.

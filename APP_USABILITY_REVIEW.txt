Local app improvements - 2026-10-13

Built on the tested TestFlight 1.1 (1045) source.
- Before you leave extends offline readiness with translation language status,
  preparation navigation, notification settings and a final airplane-mode check.
- Saved translations support optional titles, search, confirmed deletion and
  persistent undo of the most recent deletion during the current app session.
  Older translations without titles remain readable.
- Translation editing uses scrollable sections, accessible fields and save retries.
- Home offers first-trip guidance without forcing an onboarding modal; Getting
  started remains available from Settings after dismissal.
- My Trip shows today's bookings and the current/next booking with timing labels.
  Long-press a booking to open its attached documents directly; existing visible
  document shortcuts remain available from Home and the booking editor.
- Privacy policy and Support both open the user-specified URL:
  https://irvineapps.wordpress.com/
- Help and Home use the same Before you leave label.
- Translation editing prompts Save changes, Discard changes or Keep editing
  before Back navigation, and disables sheet-swipe dismissal while edits exist.
- Repeated Save taps for unchanged translation content reuse its identifier.
- Checklist checks restart on return-navigation and foreground activation,
  with cancellation guards preventing old results from changing current status.
- First-trip guidance appears after the main Home information.

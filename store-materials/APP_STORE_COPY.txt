Tama World Clock — English (U.S.) listing copy
Local branding draft for version 1.2. Short app name: Tama Clock.

NAME
Tama World Clock

SUBTITLE
Clocks, currency & trip notes

PROMOTIONAL TEXT
Keep clocks, currency, bookings and documents together. Translate useful text and check what is saved before you head offline.

DESCRIPTION
Keep the details of your journey close at hand.

Tama World Clock brings world clocks, currency conversions, travel notes, documents and translation into one place on your iPhone. From checking the time back home to finding a saved confirmation, your everyday travel tools are easy to reach.

COMPARE TIMES
See Home and Destination clocks together, compare cities, and check booking times in their departure and arrival time zones.

MAKE SENSE OF PRICES
Convert currencies, total multiple prices, and read marked prices from a supported live camera or a selected photo. Save conversions with notes and reference photos.

KEEP YOUR TRIP ORGANIZED
Save flights, hotel stays and transport details, including routes, booking references and notes. Add optional departure or check-in reminders and see today's and upcoming travel entries.

KEEP DOCUMENTS HANDY
Import PDFs and images, label them, assign them to a booking, and search by trip or traveler. Saved readable documents remain available offline. Travel entries, drafts and imported documents are encrypted locally.

TRANSLATE AND SAVE
Translate entered text or text recognized from a camera or photo using supported Apple language pairs. Save useful translations with a title, notes and a reference image. Prepare supported languages while connected for offline translation.

CHECK BEFORE YOU LEAVE
Review saved travel details, documents, reference rates, weather and language availability before departure. Freshness labels help distinguish current information from older saved data.

No app account is required. Tama World Clock does not provide its own cloud synchronization. Optional app lock uses your iPhone's authentication; widgets are outside app lock.

Currency conversions are estimates based on dated reference rates. Bank rates and fees may differ. Fresh rates, weather, new weather-location searches and language downloads require internet. Camera scanning depends on device support and permission. Reminder delivery depends on iPhone notification and Focus settings. Encrypted travel information uses a device-specific key, so restoring files to a different device may not restore access.

REVIEW NOTES
No app account or login is required. Version 1.1 (1048).
Home compares clocks and includes a currency converter. Bottom tabs open Home, Time, Translate and My Trip. Full converter opens multiple prices and camera/photo price recognition. Home’s More menu opens Weather, Currency, Documents, Before you leave and Settings. My Trip supports manual booking entries and optional reminders; the folder button opens Documents for PDF/image imports. Translate supports entered text and camera/photo recognition with Apple's supported language pairs. Language preparation may download Apple language assets. Settings contains App guide, built-in Privacy & support, and About Tama World Clock.
Optional app lock uses device authentication. Widgets are not protected by app lock. Reminder notifications omit private booking references and destinations. No booking-service credentials are needed. Use your own sample PDF or image to test document import. The store screenshots contain example travel data; the installed app begins with the user's own data.

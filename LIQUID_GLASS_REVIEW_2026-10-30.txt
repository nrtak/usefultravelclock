Approved Liquid Glass styling, implemented locally 2026-10-30.

Changes:
- Native Home/My Trip/Tools tab bar uses the system appearance on iOS 26 and later. Minimization is disabled so all three tabs stay available.
- Toolbar glass is no longer suppressed by the shared toolbar helper. Home Customize/Settings and existing Save/Cancel/Done controls use the native toolbar button style on iOS 26 and later.
- Key actions (saving currency/unit conversions, translation, saved-item actions and scan-result actions) use Apple's glass/glassProminent button styles with capsule shapes. Native toolbar buttons do not add a second glass effect.
- Information cards retain their opaque backgrounds. Home's card capacity still comes from the actual available area and text size, with no scroll container.
- iOS 18 remains the deployment target. Every iOS 26 API is guarded; earlier releases use the existing solid button style with at least 44-point hit dimensions.
- The native controls provide system accessibility adaptations; no custom shimmer, blur animation, timer or background work was added. Actual accessibility appearance and battery use require device verification.
